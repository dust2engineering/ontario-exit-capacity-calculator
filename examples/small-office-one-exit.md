# Example 2 — Small office, Group D, not sprinklered

Input: 40 occupants, floor area 180 m2, longest travel 20 m, not sprinklered.

- Aggregate exit width through doorways/corridors: 40 x 6.1 = 244 mm (minimum door widths still govern each door)
- Maximum travel distance, Group D not sprinklered: 40 m — 20 m is OK
- One exit? Table 3.4.2.1.A for Group D: max area 200 m2, max travel 25 m, load 40 <= 60 — a single
  exit may be permitted if the building is not more than 2 storeys and every other condition of
  Sentence 3.4.2.1.(2) is met.
