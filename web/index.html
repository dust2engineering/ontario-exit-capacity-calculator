Ontario Building Code 3.4.3.2 sets the aggregate width of exits from the occupant load: 6.1 mm per person for doorways, corridors and passageways, 8 mm per person for stairs with a rise of not more than 180 mm and a run of not less than 280 mm, and 9.2 mm per person for other stairs. A single exit is only possible inside the small limits of Tables 3.4.2.1.A and 3.4.2.1.B, in a building of not more than 2 storeys with an occupant load of not more than 60. Maximum travel distances are in Table 3.4.2.2.
